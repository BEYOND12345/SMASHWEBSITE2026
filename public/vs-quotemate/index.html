Why “AI quoting” still fails without your catalog

The founder story is simple: ChatGPT and Claude can write a beautiful quote and still get the money wrong. Same risk with any AI that invents labour or materials. SMASH was built after that failure — see ChatGPT got my estimates wrong.

If QuoteMate (or any AI quoter) helps you draft faster but you still do not trust the dollars, you need catalog fidelity: AI estimates from your rates.
