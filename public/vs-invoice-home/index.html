Invoice Home is a classic “make a nice invoice PDF” tool — templates, branding, download, email. SMASH assumes the bottleneck is not the PDF. It is capturing the job and sending a priced document before you drive off.

Template traffic is switcher traffic

People land on Invoice Home when they search free invoice templates. Many of them are the same ICP as SMASH — sole traders who still batch admin on Sunday. The upgrade path is not a prettier template. It is voice to invoice so the document leaves with you.

Pricing note

Invoice Home offers free and paid template tiers — confirm on their site. SMASH free plan includes a monthly send allowance; paid unlocks volume and sync. Pricing.

Who SMASH is right for

Anyone using Invoice Home (or Word/Excel) who wants to stop rebuilding invoices after every job.

When Invoice Home is enough

  • You need a one-off PDF and will never invoice from a job site
  • You only bill a few times a year

For weekly trade volume, templates become the paperwork tax.
