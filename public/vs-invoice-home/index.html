Template traffic is switcher traffic

People land on Invoice Home searching free invoice templates. Many are the same ICP as SMASH — sole traders who still batch admin on Sunday, or still use Word vs Excel. The upgrade is not a prettier template. It is voice to invoice so the document leaves with you.

What a handyman actually needs

After a fence job you need a priced quote the owner can approve on their phone — not a blank template waiting on a laptop. If they say yes, the invoice should be the same lines. If they pay on the link, you are done before the next job. Template makers skip most of that loop. So do a lot of “simple” apps — see Zoho Invoice and Wave for the same pattern.
