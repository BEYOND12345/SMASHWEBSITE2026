Short answer

Invoice Home makes nice invoice PDFs. Templates are not a trade workflow.

Fair review: if you need a branded document occasionally, Invoice Home (and tools like it) do that job. If you are a cleaner or handyman with weekly volume, the pain is capturing the job and getting paid — not picking a prettier layout.

Keep / switch

Keep if you bill rarely and only need a template. Look past if Sunday admin is the real cost — same story as Zoho Invoice and Wave.

A real handyman week vs a template app

Monday: flywire on three windows. Tuesday: flat-pack and a gate latch. Wednesday: property manager list — six small jobs. None of those wait politely while you open a template, retype labour, guess materials, export a PDF, and email it later.

That is why template apps feel fine in a demo and fail in a ute. The document is not the hard part. Remembering the job and sending before you leave is.

If you are still bouncing between Word, Excel, and Invoice Home, read Word vs Excel vs app for invoices — then ask whether you need another template or a process.

What “good enough” costs

A $120 job you forget to invoice is not a software bug. It is friction. Multiply by a month of “I’ll do it Sunday” and you are funding someone else’s tools with unbilled work. Same story we see with Invoice Simple and Invoice2go — different brands, same keyboard tax.

Bottom line

Invoice Home is honest about being a document tool. Our ICP usually needs a process. Pick for the problem you have.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Invoice Home good for tradies?

For occasional branded PDFs, yes. For weekly on-site estimates and same-day invoicing with dirty hands, templates become the bottleneck.

What is the biggest Invoice Home limitation?

It is a document maker, not a driveway process. Capture, approval, and payment are not the product the way speed-to-send is.

Is Invoice Home free?

They offer free and paid template tiers that change — confirm on Invoice Home’s site.

What should I use instead of Invoice Home for trade volume?

Something that captures the job on site from your rates and finishes approve → invoice → pay. See our Invoice Home comparison.

Can Invoice Home replace Word or Excel?

For nicer PDFs, often yes. For stopping Sunday admin after six jobs, you still need a real workflow — see Word vs Excel vs app.
