  • Free invoices that wait until Sunday still cost real cash flow.
  • Keep Wave for books if you want; change the capture tool if the driveway is where quotes die.

Short answer

Wave is a great free desk invoicer. Free does not fix evenings.

Honest review: Wave helps freelancers invoice without a big software bill. That is genuinely good. The pain for cleaners, handymen, and mobile trades is different — the job happens in driveways, not browsers, and the money loop needs to finish before you leave.

Deep comparison: SMASH vs Wave · Related Gmail angle: Wave invoicing alternative from Gmail · Voice to invoice.

What Wave does well

  • Price — free core invoicing is a real reason people start here after Excel
  • Accounting adjacent — receipts, books, and invoices in one freelancer-friendly world
  • Clean invoices — professional enough for most small clients
  • Low commitment — easy to try without a sales call

If you invoice from a laptop twice a week, Wave can be the right tool for years.

Where Wave breaks for people on the tools

Free invoices, paid Sundays

Wave removes the subscription wall. It does not remove typing. Six small jobs × “I’ll Wave it tonight” is how cash sits on the truck. Average uninvoiced work is a habit problem — tools that make tonight optional lose.

Desk-first vs driveway-first

Wave assumes a browser or a calm moment. Customers ask for prices while you are packing tools. The product that wins that moment is the one that speaks and sends — see send a quote on site in ~30 seconds.

Invoice ≠ approve ≠ paid

Wave can invoice and take payments. The loop many trades need is quote → customer approves → invoice same lines → paid, with a read receipt. Scorecard: vs Wave.

Keep Wave if…

  • You need free books and rarely quote on site
  • Your clients are remote and admin happens at a desk
  • Wave’s banking/receipts workflow is already home

Look past Wave (for capture) if…

  • You are a handyman, cleaner, or mobile trade with daily volume
  • Quotes die because you are still typing later
  • You want the customer to approve while they are still there

Fair setup many people use

SMASH for speed on site or in Gmail. Accounting tool of choice for books. That is not “Wave is bad.” That is two jobs. Paid SMASH syncs Xero/QuickBooks; Wave can remain a chapter in the story.

Bottom line

Wave wins on free desk invoicing. Tradies lose money when the process stops at “type it later.” Fix the capture and loop — keep the books wherever they belong.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Wave good for freelancers?

Yes — especially if you want free or low-cost invoicing and basic accounting at a desk. It is a weaker fit when most of your quoting happens on job sites with dirty hands.

What is the biggest Wave pain point for tradies?

Desk-first workflow. You still type line items later. That is how “I’ll Wave it tonight” becomes uninvoiced work.

Is Wave free?

Core invoicing has long been free; payments and extras can cost money. Always check Wave’s current pricing — it changes.

Can I use Wave and a faster quoting tool together?

Yes. Many people capture and send with a speed tool on site, then keep or sync accounting separately. Paid SMASH plans sync Xero and QuickBooks.
