Short answer

Joist is a proper contractor app. You are still typing.

This review is for sole traders and small crews comparing Joist — not a teardown. Joist earned its reputation with estimates, invoices, and payments aimed at contractors. The honest question is whether your bottleneck is missing features… or the form between the finished job and a sent number.

Deep comparison: SMASH vs Joist · Voice to invoice · First quote wins.

What Joist does well

  • Contractor-shaped product — estimates and invoices are first-class, not bolted on
  • Path to getting paid — payments matter; Joist takes that seriously
  • Familiar mobile workflow — if you like structured line items, it feels purpose-built
  • Serious alternative to “Excel + hope” — a real upgrade for many trades

If you live in Joist and it is working, that is a valid outcome. Switching for fashion is dumb.

Where Joist breaks for dirty-hands work

The open tab problem

Customer asks for a price. You open Joist. You start tapping. Phone rings. You save a draft. Draft becomes tonight. Tonight becomes never. The app did not fail — capture speed failed.

Feature-rich ≠ driveway-fast

Joist can do a lot. Handymen often need to do one thing extremely fast: priced PDF → approve → invoice → pay. Extra menus do not help when hands are full. See for handymen.

Forms vs voice

If the competitor category is “contractor apps,” the differentiator is input. Tap forms vs talk against your rates. Same ICP as Invoice Simple switchers.

Keep Joist if…

  • Client history and payments are deep in Joist and switching cost is high
  • You prefer Joist’s UI and do not want voice
  • You mostly build estimates at a desk anyway

Look past Joist if…

  • “Type it later” is your default after every job
  • You lose work because someone else sent a PDF first
  • You want approval + pay on the same customer link after a voice send

Bottom line

Joist is good software for contractors who will use forms. Sole traders on the tools often need a faster process, not another menu. Be clear which problem you are solving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Joist good for sole traders?

Yes if you want contractor-focused estimates, invoices, and payments in one app and you are comfortable building jobs by tapping. It is less ideal when you need a priced PDF out in under a minute with dirty hands.

What is the biggest Joist pain point on site?

Time to build the estimate. Features are there; the keyboard is still the bottleneck between the customer asking “how much?” and a PDF in their hand.

Does Joist replace full job-management software?

For many solos, Joist covers estimates and getting paid without a heavy suite. Larger crews often still want scheduling tools alongside invoicing.

What is a fair Joist alternative?

Look for the same contractor outcomes — estimate, approve, invoice, pay — with faster capture. SMASH uses voice against your catalog; see the Joist comparison page.

Should I migrate off Joist completely?

Only if the pain is daily. Many people trial a faster capture tool for new jobs while keeping Joist history until they are ready.
