Short answer

Invoice Simple is a solid tap-to-type invoicer. It was not designed for the full dirty-hands money loop.

This is my honest review as someone who built software after living the trade-admin problem — not a hit piece. Invoice Simple helps a lot of freelancers and contractors send professional invoices from a phone. What it does not remove is the keyboard tax between jobs, or the gap between “PDF sent” and “customer approved and paid while I’m still on site.”

What Invoice Simple does well

Credit where it is due:

  • Mobile-first invoices — built for phone use, not a desktop afterthought
  • Estimates → invoices — you can convert work without starting from zero every time
  • Low learning curve — templates and fields most people understand in a day
  • Good fit for low volume — one or two invoices a week and typing is annoying, not catastrophic

If that is your business, Invoice Simple can be the right tool. Plenty of sole traders are happy there.

Where it breaks for people who work with their hands

1. Typing is the product

Invoice Simple’s workflow assumes you can tap descriptions, quantities, and rates. On a handyman afternoon — flat-pack, gate latch, three odd jobs for a property manager — that means five forms. The work is done. The paperwork waits.

2. “Invoice sent” ≠ process finished

A PDF in someone’s inbox is not the same as: they approved the scope, you converted to invoice, they paid, you saw they opened it. Many basic apps stop at send. The full loop is what service businesses actually run.

3. Your rates live in your head (or get retyped)

Catalog memory matters when every job is different. Re-entering the same call-out and hourly is how inconsistency creeps in — and how ChatGPT-style guesses sneak into quotes. We wrote about that failure mode here: ChatGPT got my estimates wrong.

A real week on the tools

Monday: flywire. Tuesday: flat-pack plus Bunnings bits. Wednesday: six small jobs for a property manager. None of those wait while you rebuild line items on a phone. That is why “simple” still becomes Sunday admin for handymen — same ICP story as Invoice2go and Joist.

Switching smart (without overnight migrations)

You do not need to export five years of history on day one. Keep Invoice Simple for old clients if you want. Run the next week of on-site quotes through a faster capture trial. Compare: minutes from “how much?” to PDF sent, and whether the customer approved before you left.

If that experiment wins, migrate gradually. If it does not, you lost an afternoon — not your whole admin stack. Same advice we give on Invoice2go and Joist: trial the pain, do not rage-quit a working PDF tool.

Who should keep Invoice Simple

  • You send a handful of invoices a month
  • You almost never need an on-site estimate
  • Typing on a phone does not bother you
  • You do not need customer approval as part of the same motion

Who should look past it

  • Handymen, cleaners, gardeners, mobile trades with several jobs a day
  • Anyone whose backlog is “I’ll invoice Sunday”
  • Anyone who loses jobs because the quote left too late — first quote wins

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Invoice Simple good for tradies?

Yes for sole traders who want a simple phone invoicer and are fine building line items by tapping. It is weaker when you need fast on-site estimates with dirty hands and a customer approval link in the same motion.

What is the biggest Invoice Simple pain point on site?

Time. Five jobs means five rounds of typing descriptions, quantities, and tax. That is when “I’ll finish invoices tonight” starts — and tonight often never comes.

Does Invoice Simple do estimates and invoices?

Yes — estimates and invoices are core. The gap for many trades is speed of capture and whether approval and payment feel like one loop after the job, not a separate admin project.

What is a fair Invoice Simple alternative?

If your pain is typing or delayed sends, look for voice or catalog-priced capture that still lets the customer approve and pay. SMASH is built for that loop — see our comparison page.

Should I leave Invoice Simple immediately?

No. Start free on a new tool for the next quote, keep Invoice Simple until you trust the workflow. Switching overnight is rarely necessary.
