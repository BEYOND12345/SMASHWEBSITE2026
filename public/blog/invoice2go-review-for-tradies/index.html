Where it breaks for people who work with their hands

1. Caps vs trade volume

A handyman can burn a “two invoices a month” style plan before Tuesday lunch. Paying up for unlimited while still typing every line is the classic fork. Numbers move — verify on Invoice2go — but the shape of the problem stays: cheap plans under-serve busy solos.

2. Typing is still the product

Invoice2go is forms. Good forms. Forms on a phone after a gutter clean still take minutes you do not have. SMASH’s bet is talk for ~30 seconds against your rates, verify, send. Same category (mobile quoting/invoicing), different input.

3. PDF sent ≠ process finished

Estimates can be approved in many apps. The loop trades need is: priced quote while they wait → approve → invoice same lines → pay → you know they opened it. Scorecard lives on the vs page.

Keep Invoice2go if…

  • You send one or two invoices a month for real
  • You like their templates / mini-site extras
  • Typing does not bother you
  • You are deep in their ecosystem and switching cost is high

Look past it if…

  • You quote multiple times a week (handymen, cleaners, gardeners)
  • You keep hitting plan limits
  • Quotes die because you are still typing in the ute — first quote wins

Fair alternative

If the pain is capture + volume + loop, try voice against your catalog, then let the customer approve and pay. That is SMASH vs Invoice2go. Start free; keep Invoice2go until you are sure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Invoice2go good for tradies?

It makes professional invoices from a phone. For trade volumes, the cheap plans’ invoice caps are tight, and every document is still typed. Fine for very low volume; painful for several jobs a week.

How many invoices can you send on Invoice2go?

Plans change — historically Starter was only a couple of invoices per month on monthly billing, with higher tiers unlocking more. Always check Invoice2go’s current pricing page before you decide.

What is the biggest Invoice2go pain on site?

Capture speed. Forms on a phone between jobs. That is when estimates wait until tonight and jobs get forgotten.

What is a fair Invoice2go alternative?

If the pain is typing or caps, look for unlimited-enough workflow with voice or catalog pricing and customer approval on the send. See our SMASH vs Invoice2go page.

Should I leave Invoice2go overnight?

No. Trial the next quote on a faster tool, keep Invoice2go until you trust the loop.
